# Cloud Migration Assistant: storyline

Source: the internship deck, `~/Downloads/atlassian /merged.pdf` (51 slides, image only). Product design internship on the
Migrations team at Atlassian, two months, remote, 2023. Internal name: The Trust Project.

## NDA rules for this page

The deck was shared for closed-group recruitment viewing. The page is public, so:

- Every product screen is redrawn in HTML with placeholder data (project names, sizes, counts, site URL).
- No staging URLs, internal dashboards, OKR details or real customer numbers.
- Process, research framing, pain points and design decisions stay, in plain words.

## Sections

1. Hero: title, one-line lede, facts, interactive Choose screen (final design).
2. Context: Server and Data Center to Cloud, and where the assistant sits.
3. Problem: the interpreted problem statement and the five questions it raised (each links to its section).
4. Process: four phases and what happened in each.
5. Trust: the car story, the three pillars (predictable, reliable, transparent), confidence, trust.
6. Users: customers by size and complexity (focus: complex enterprise); three admin roles.
7. Pain points: five sources, seven pain points, low confidence in tooling chosen.
8. Migration journey: a matrix of the six stages, with what happens, the work, where the assistant is used and where my
   three journeys sit.
9. Three journeys: where each sits in the assistant.
10. Journey 1, creating a plan: the current flow as a walkthrough (five redrawn old screens, what each step does, its
    pain points with numbered markers on the screen), a trust audit bar chart, six pages to one, feedback from dev, PM
    and data science, ideation timeline, annotated final screen.
11. Journey 2, first impressions: before and after home, treemap overview, resource monitor, security links.
12. Journey 3, changing a saved plan: the dashboard scenario (Q3 move: seven projects, five migrated, two failed), the
    problem (backwards, repeated steps, no record of what moved) on the old flow, then each problem beside its fix, and
    the working edit screen (trimmed selection, restore, recommended plan).
13. What I learned, outcome (no metrics yet), thanks, next project.

The pillar tag on each pain point in the trust audit is a reading added for this page, not a slide from the deck.
