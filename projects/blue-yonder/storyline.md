# Sustainable Supply Chain — storyline

Source: `~/Downloads/Blue yonder, sustainable supply chain/merged pdf.pdf` (10 pages, Team 11).
Blue Yonder's brief at Inter IIT Tech Meet. Edition and result not in the report; confirm with Krunal.

## Order

Hero: title, lede, facts. Fig 1 carbon map: the report's chain of 12 stages, circle size = relative emissions as drawn
in the report (measured from the PDF figure). Solution pills light the stages each one acts on (my mapping).

Brief
- Brief: pandemic, speed and cost over carbon; five-year plan; COP26 targets (added, sourced).
- Scale: 35 Gt global CO2; China 11,680, US 4,535, India 2,412 (report says "metric tonnes", these are million
  tonnes); 30% GMV lost to cancellations and returns; 660,788 t plastic waste in India a year.
- Four domains: warehousing, transportation, returns, packaging process maps (report page 7).

Problems
- Nine problems, read by lens: impact, cost, CO2, customer (report pages 2 and 6).
- Priorities: problems to solutions (my mapping).

Solutions (report pages 3, 4, 5, 8, 10)
- Last mile optimisation: reply flow, illustrative delivery day.
- Warehouse and inventory automation: WMS system, pick path demo (exact routing on a toy layout).
- Sustainable tertiary packaging: truck load, volume share 14.43% pallets -> ~0.9% slip sheets (99.1% for cartons).
- Convertible delivery bag: flip, box and bag loops.
- Systematic waste management: disposition by category, plastic road calculator (1 t/km, 3.5 t credits/km).

Plan (report page 9)
- Timeline: gantt, years 0-5.
- Rollout: grade and type shares. Grade C is 15% (report prints 35%; totals only add up at 15%).
  Tertiary packaging uses the table split 14 / 24.75 / 61.25 (report's gantt shows 14 / 28.5 / 57.5).
- Impact: all metrics in one table.
- Outcome: report conclusion.

Left out: "saves 99.1% of volume" wording (reworded as cartons get 99.1% of the space).
